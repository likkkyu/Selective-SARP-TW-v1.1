Two-panel scientific figure. Panel a maps one frozen 50-order scenario on the actual Futian–Luohu directed OSM driving graph, with depot, pickup and delivery nodes and eight strict-insertion vehicle routes drawn along road shortest paths. Panel b plots mean served orders against algorithm time for neural construction, neural plus three-second LNS, strict insertion, and insertion plus three-second LNS. Pure neural construction has the lowest service rate; both LNS variants approach 100 percent service at approximately 3.1–3.25 seconds.
